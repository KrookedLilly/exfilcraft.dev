Server config

Every ExfilCraft config option with its default.

Server rules

Gameplay rules. They live in config/exfilcraft-server.toml, a copy in a world's serverconfig folder overrides them for that world, and every player who joins gets the server's values. File: exfilcraft-server.toml

Ammunition

Tipped ammunition: how a tipped arrow or bolt spreads its potion. What a tipped shot does depends on the bottle it was tipped from at the fletching table. Tipped from a drinkable potion, it only affects what it hits. Tipped from a splash potion, it bursts where it lands the way a thrown splash potion does, so everyone close enough gets the effect and the closer they are the longer it lasts. Tipped from a lingering potion, it bursts the same way and also leaves a small cloud on the ground for 10 seconds. Whatever it hits directly still takes the full effect, and the burst works on a miss too.

OptionDefaultWhat it does
Tipped Arrows Splash
tippedArrowSplash		trueWhether arrows and bolts tipped from splash or lingering potions burst on impact. Turn it off and every tipped shot only affects what it hits, with no burst and no cloud.
Tipped Splash Potency
tippedArrowSplashPotency		1
0 to 8		How strong the burst is compared to the arrow's own tip, where 1 is the same strength. An arrow's tip is deliberately weaker than a bottle, so raise this if you want a tipped shot to hit as hard as a splash potion does.
Tipped Splash Radius
tippedArrowSplashRadius		4
0.5 to 16		How far the burst reaches, in blocks. A thrown splash potion reaches 4.

Auction House

Auction house: the player-to-player marketplace opened from the Plot Trader and the Faction Officer. One shared marketplace for the whole server, open to every player. Sellers put an item up with either a fixed price, an open invitation for offers, or both; the item is held by the auction house until it sells, is cancelled, or expires. Nothing is ever pushed back into an inventory: goods, payments and returned offers all wait in the seller's or bidder's Claims tab.

Enchanting

Enchanting: how many enchantments a piece of gear can hold, and what the merchant bench charges. Enchantments reach gear ONLY through the five category merchants now (weaponsmith/toolsmith/ armorer/fletcher/fisherman); the anvil repairs and renames, and the librarian merges books. A slot is one DISTINCT enchantment whatever its level, so upgrading Sharpness III to IV is free of slot cost. Looted gear deliberately ignores these caps: a found item may carry more than its material allows, it just cannot be added to.

Wanted Status

Wanted status: what happens to a player who kills another player in a raid. A killer is marked Wanted, their name turns red, and other players can buy a Wanted Contract naming them and hunt them for a reward. Killing a player who is already Wanted does not mark you, and a kill by somebody on the victim's friends list does nothing at all, so friends cannot stage clean resets for each other.
